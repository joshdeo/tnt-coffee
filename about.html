About TNT Cup Coffee

A neighbourhood retreat built around the cup

Premium-quality brews, a calm room and a team that makes every guest feel like part of the family.

Nestled in the heart of Mississauga at 25 Dundas St W, TNT Cup Coffee has established itself as a premier destination for specialty coffee enthusiasts and those seeking a welcoming neighbourhood retreat. It seamlessly blends premium-quality brews with a cozy, inviting ambiance, making it the perfect sanctuary for focused studying, remote work, or catching up with friends.

The space has a sleek, contemporary design flooded with soft natural light, accented by warm wooden textures and lush greenery that instantly evoke a sense of calm.

The cafe is highly celebrated for its curated selection of artisanal coffee, crafted from top-tier beans, from rich, velvety espressos and classic cappuccinos to innovative, handcrafted iced beverages.

  • Fast Wi-Fi
  • Soft natural light
  • Warm wood and greenery
  • Made for studying and remote work

Best known for

  • Freshly baked artisanal pastries

  • Warm and welcoming hospitality

    A passionate team of skilled baristas dedicated to providing friendly, top-notch service that makes every guest feel like part of the family.

Whether you are searching for that perfect espresso to kickstart your morning routine, or a peaceful corner to conquer your to-do list alongside a fresh pastry, TNT Cup Coffee provides a warm, uplifting experience that fuels both the body and mind.
